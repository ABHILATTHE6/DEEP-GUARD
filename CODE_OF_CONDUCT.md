# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors are expected to be respectful, constructive, and professional. Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Project-specific responsibility

DEEP-Guard concerns synthetic-media detection. Discussions should distinguish experimental model results from established facts and should document limitations rather than overclaiming detection certainty.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when conduct violates this code.
